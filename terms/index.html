2. What Pallet Quote does

Pallet Quote is a quoting calculator and drawing tool for timber pallets. You enter a pallet size, choose timber and set your own rates. It works out board counts, gaps and cost, and produces a quote and a drawing as a PDF.

The timber list and prices that come with Pallet Quote are examples to get you started. They are not market prices and are not a recommendation of what to charge.
