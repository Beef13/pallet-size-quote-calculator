Where the time goes

A pallet quote is nine jobs. You do three.

Once your rates are in, Pallet Quoter takes the six that eat the time, and does them the same way every time.

  1. 1 Take the size and quantityFrom the customer, on the phone or in person. You
  2. 2 Choose the timberBoards and bearers, from your own list. You
  3. 3 Work out how many boards fit, and the gapsCounts that won't fit aren't allowed. Pallet Quoter
  4. 4 Measure every board and bearerCut to the real length of the pallet. Pallet Quoter
  5. 5 Cost the timber by the metreAt your price for each size, plus nails. Pallet Quoter
  6. 6 Add labour, markup and GSTYour figures, applied the same way on every quote. Pallet Quoter
  7. 7 Draw the pallet to scalePlan, elevations and dimensions for the customer to approve. Pallet Quoter
  8. 8 Write up the quote and keep your costingA customer PDF and your own breakdown, under one quote number. Pallet Quoter
  9. 9 Send itEmail or print the customer PDF. You

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up?

Enter your price per metre for the timber sizes you use, your labour per pallet and your markup. That's done once; after it, each quote is a size, a timber and a quantity. The calculator comes with common green case pine and kiln dried sizes, and you can edit the list.

What does the price include?

Timber for every board and bearer by the metre at your rates, nails, labour per pallet, your markup and GST. Freight, heat treatment, a waste allowance and a minimum order charge are not yet included, so allow for those separately.

Will my customer see my rates or margin?

No. The customer PDF shows the drawing, the specification and the price. Your rates, labour, markup and profit are only on your own breakdown.

Is any installation required?

No. Pallet Quoter runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and keeps working offline once it's loaded.

What does it cost?

The calculator is free, with no account and no limit on the number of quotes. Pro is $49 per month or $490 per year in Australian dollars and adds an account, online backup and synchronisation across your devices.

Where is my data stored?

In the browser on your device; no account is required. If you log in with your email address, your prices and quotes are also backed up online and synchronised across your devices.

In the browser on your device. No data is transmitted elsewhere. Use Back up to save a copy to a file, as clearing your browser data also removes your prices and quotes.
