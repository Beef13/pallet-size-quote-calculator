From a size to a signed-off quote

Four steps, one screen each.

  1. Choose the timber and sizes for the bottom boards, bearers and top deck. The pallet takes shape in 3D as you go, to scale, with every gap worked out and dimensioned.

    • Won't let you fit more boards than the width can take
    • Leader boards on the outside edges, priced on their own line
    • Drag to turn it round and show the customer exactly what they're getting

  2. Every board and bearer is costed on its real length at your own rate per metre. Add your labour per pallet and your markup, and GST goes on top. Change the size or the quantity and the price moves with it.

    • Markup and the gross margin it gives you, side by side, so the two never get mixed up
    • Prices stay locked until you unlock them, so nobody bumps a rate by accident
    • Every line shows the count, the size and the length it was priced on

  3. Every quote comes with a proper shop drawing: plan, front and side elevations at true scale, an isometric view, and an enlarged end detail with every thickness marked. What they sign is what you build.

    • Parts lettered to match the specification table
    • Board widths, gaps, bearer spacing and overall height, all dimensioned
    • Third-angle projection with a title block, scale and notes

  4. The customer gets a clean quote: your business details, the drawings, the specification and the total with GST. You keep the breakdown: every metre, your markup and your profit. Same quote number on both.

    • The customer copy shows what they're buying and what it costs. Your rates stay with you
    • Every quote is saved with the rates it was priced on, so last month's quote doesn't shift when timber does
    • Repeat order? Duplicate the old quote at today's rates

The small things that save the most time

  • Saved presets

    Save the pallets you quote every week. Load one, change the quantity, and the quote's done.

  • Locked prices

    Your rates don't move until you unlock them. Update them when the timber invoice changes, then lock them again.

  • Leader boards

    Wider or thicker boards on the outside edges. The gaps are recalculated and each size gets its own line.

  • Works without signal

    Once it's open, it keeps working in the yard, the truck or the back paddock. Quote on the spot and send it when you're back in range.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open it in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Can I use my own timber prices?

Yes. Every board and bearer size has its own price per metre, plus a price per nail. Unlock a price, change it and save. Your prices stay on your device and can be exported to a file and imported on another one.

Which timber and sizes does it cover?

It comes set up with green case pine and kiln dried boards and bearers in the common sizes, from 100 × 12 up to 150 × 25 for boards. Any width and length of pallet works.

Does the price include my labour and profit?

Yes, once you set them. Enter your labour per pallet and your markup under Prices and every quote includes them. The quote shows your markup next to the gross margin it works out to, so the two never get mixed up. Freight isn't included yet.

Will my customer understand the drawing?

Yes. The customer copy shows a plan, two elevations, a 3D view and an enlarged end detail, with each part lettered to match the list underneath. Nothing is left to interpret.

What does the customer see, and what stays with me?

The customer PDF shows your business details, the drawings, the parts and sizes, the price per pallet, GST and the total. Your rates, labour, markup and line-by-line costs only appear on the breakdown PDF, which is for your own records.

Where is my data kept?

Prices, presets and saved quotes are stored in the browser on your device, and the calculator works without an account. If you sign in with your email, they are also backed up online and kept in step across your devices. You can save a copy to a file at any time with Back up.
