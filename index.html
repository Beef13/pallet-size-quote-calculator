Seven boards at 1.165 m, three bearers at 1.165 m, times the rate per metre, plus nails. Then the customer asks what it would cost at 1140.

A drawing on the back of a docket that only you can read, sent to a customer who won't sign off what they can't picture.

The wait

Working it out by hand takes the evening. By the time the quote goes out, the customer has already asked someone else.

Pallet Quote does all three in a few minutes.

A full quote in six short steps

This is the calculator itself, making one quote for 250 pallets. Scroll to play it through.

  1. Step 1

    Type the size.

    Width and length, or load a preset for the pallets you make every week. The pallet is outlined to scale as soon as you finish typing.

  2. Step 2

    Pick the timber, part by part.

    Bottom boards, bearers, top boards. Each one appears in the 3D model as you choose it, with its cost beside it, priced on its real length at your rate per metre.

  3. Step 3

    Change your mind as often as you like.

    Add a board and the rest re-space themselves: the gaps go from 78 mm to 33, and the price follows. Take it back off just as quickly. It never lets you fit more boards than the width allows.

  4. Step 4

    The price is already worked out.

    Set your labour and markup once. Every quote adds them, multiplies by the quantity and puts GST on top, and shows the margin your markup works out to.

  5. Step 5

    So is the drawing.

    Plan, front and side elevations at true scale, an isometric view and an enlarged end detail, each part lettered to match the list. Nothing to sketch.

  6. Step 6

    Two PDFs, numbered and saved.

    One for the customer with the drawings and the total. One for you with every metre, your markup and your profit. Both are kept in History, where you mark each quote sent, accepted or lost.

The small things that save the most time

  • Saved presets

    Save the pallets you quote every week and load one with a click. Change the quantity and the quote is done.

  • Locked prices

    Your rates stay put until you unlock them. Update them when the timber invoice changes, then lock them again.

  • Leader boards

    Wider or thicker boards on the outside edges, with the gaps recalculated and each size priced on its own line.

  • Works without signal

    Once it's opened, it keeps working in the yard, the truck or the back paddock. Quote now, send it when you're back in range.

  • Phone, tablet or desk

    The same calculator on whatever's in your hand. Sign in with your email and your prices and quotes follow you from one device to the next.

  • Every quote on file

    Quotes are numbered and saved as you send them. Mark them sent, accepted or lost, search by customer, and duplicate one for a repeat order.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open it in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Can I use my own timber prices?

Yes. Every board and bearer size has its own price per metre, plus a price per nail. Unlock a price, change it and save. Your prices stay on your device and can be exported to a file and imported on another one.

Which timber and sizes does it cover?

It comes set up with green case pine and kiln dried boards and bearers in the common sizes, from 100 × 12 up to 150 × 25 for boards. Any width and length of pallet works.

Does the price include my labour and profit?

Yes, once you set them. Enter your labour per pallet and your markup under Prices and every quote includes them. The quote shows the markup and the gross margin it works out to, so there's no confusion between the two. Freight isn't included yet.

Will my customer understand the drawing?

That's what it's for. The customer copy shows a plan, two elevations, a 3D view and an enlarged end detail, with each part lettered to match the list underneath. There's nothing to interpret.

What does the customer see, and what stays with me?

The customer PDF shows your business details, the drawings, the parts and sizes, the price per pallet, GST and the total. Your rates, labour, markup and line-by-line costs only appear on the breakdown PDF, which is for your own records.

Where is my data kept?

Prices, presets and saved quotes are stored in the browser on your device, and the calculator works without an account. If you sign in with your email, they are also backed up online and kept in step across your devices. You can save a copy to a file at any time with Back up.
