Key features

  • Live 3D model

    The pallet is modelled to scale as each component is selected, and can be rotated to present to your customer.

  • Markup and margin

    Markup is displayed alongside the gross margin it produces, so the two are never confused.

  • Protected pricing

    Rates remain locked until you choose to edit them, which prevents accidental changes.

  • Saved presets

    Store the pallets you quote regularly and reuse them with a new quantity.

  • Quote history

    Each quote is numbered and stored with the rates it was priced on. Record it as sent, accepted or lost, or duplicate it for a repeat order.

  • Offline operation

    Once loaded, the calculator continues to work without a connection. Quotes can be sent when you are back in range.

Frequently asked questions

Is any installation required?

No. Pallet Quote runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and continues to work offline once loaded.

Can I use my own timber prices?

Yes. Each board and bearer size has its own price per metre, along with a price per nail. The calculator is supplied with green case pine and kiln dried sizes, and prices remain locked until you unlock them.

Does the price include labour and profit?

Yes, once they are set. Enter your labour per pallet and your markup, and each quote displays the markup alongside the gross margin it produces. Freight is not yet included.

Where is my data stored?

In the browser on your device; no account is required. If you log in with your email address, your prices and quotes are also backed up online and synchronised across your devices.
