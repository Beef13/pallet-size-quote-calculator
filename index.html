Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open it in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer. Once it's open it keeps working without signal.

Can I use my own timber prices?

Yes. Every board and bearer size has its own price per metre, plus a price per nail. It comes set up with green case pine and kiln dried sizes, and prices stay locked until you unlock them.

Does the price include my labour and profit?

Yes, once you set them. Enter your labour per pallet and your markup, and every quote shows the markup next to the gross margin it works out to. Freight isn't included yet.

Where is my data kept?

In the browser on your device, and the calculator works without an account. Sign in with your email and your prices and quotes are also backed up online and kept in step across your devices.
