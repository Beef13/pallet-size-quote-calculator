What happens when you start a quote

  1. It opens in your browser

    No account, no card and nothing to install. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

  2. You set your rates once

    Your price per metre for each timber size, your labour per pallet and your markup. It comes with example prices to get you started; they are not market prices, so put your own in first.

  3. You build the pallet and the price is there

    Add the customer's name and the quote gets a number, such as Q2026-0001, and is saved on your device. Mark it sent, accepted or lost, reopen it, or duplicate it for a repeat order.

  4. You export two PDFs

    Customer PDF opens your browser's print window: choose Save as PDF and email it. It has your business details, the drawing, the specification and the total with GST. Breakdown PDF is your own copy, with every rate, your markup and your profit. Both carry the same quote number.

An account is optional. Sign in with your email, with no password to remember, and your prices and quotes are backed up online and follow you from one device to the next.

There are no accounts. Your prices and quotes stay in the browser on your device. Use Back up to save a copy to a file, which you can load on another device.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing at present. There is no account to create and no card to enter. If paid plans are introduced we will say so on this page before anything is charged, and you won't be charged unless you agree to it.

Do I need an account?

No. Start a quote and it works straight away, with your prices and quotes kept in the browser on your device. An account is optional: sign in with your email and they are also backed up online and kept in step across your devices.

No. There are no accounts. Start a quote and it works straight away, with your prices and quotes kept in the browser on your device.

How do I send a quote to my customer?

Press Customer PDF. Your browser's print window opens; choose Save as PDF, then email the file or print it. The quote is saved and marked as sent. Pallet Quote does not send email itself.

What is the demo at the top of the page?

It is the real calculator with one sample pallet loaded. You can change the board counts and the quantity and look through every tab, but the size, timber and prices are fixed and it won't save a quote or export a PDF. Press Start a quote for the full calculator.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open it in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Can I use my own timber prices?

Yes. Every board and bearer size has its own price per metre, plus a price per nail. Unlock a price, change it and save. Your prices stay on your device and can be exported to a file and imported on another one.

Which timber and sizes does it cover?

It comes set up with green case pine and kiln dried boards and bearers in the common sizes, from 100 × 12 up to 150 × 25 for boards. Any width and length of pallet works.

Does the price include my labour and profit?

Yes, once you set them. Enter your labour per pallet and your markup under Prices and every quote includes them. The quote shows the markup and the gross margin it works out to, so there's no confusion between the two. Freight isn't included yet.

Will my customer understand the drawing?

That's what it's for. The customer copy shows a plan, two elevations, a 3D view and an enlarged end detail, with each part lettered to match the list underneath. There's nothing to interpret.

What does the customer see, and what stays with me?

The customer PDF shows your business details, the drawings, the parts and sizes, the price per pallet, GST and the total. Your rates, labour, markup and line-by-line costs only appear on the breakdown PDF, which is for your own records.

Where is my data kept?

Prices, presets and saved quotes are stored in the browser on your device, and the calculator works without an account. If you sign in with your email, they are also backed up online and kept in step across your devices. You can save a copy to a file at any time with Back up.

Prices, presets and saved quotes are stored in the browser on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Back up to save a copy to a file, which you can import on another device. Keep a backup, because clearing your browser data clears them too.
