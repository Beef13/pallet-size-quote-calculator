How it works

From your rates to a sent quote

Four steps, and the first is only done once.

  1. 1

    Set your rates once

    Enter your price per metre for each timber size, your labour per pallet and your markup. Rates stay locked until you choose to change them.

  2. 2

    Enter the size and select the timber

    Type the pallet dimensions, then choose the boards and bearers. The pallet is modelled in 3D as you go, and the running total updates with every change.

  3. 3

    Review the price

    Each component is itemised, followed by materials, labour, markup, GST and the total for the quantity. The markup is shown beside the gross margin it produces.

  4. 4

    Send the quote

    Export a customer PDF with the scaled drawing and the total, and keep a cost breakdown for your records. Both carry the same quote number.

Features

Built for pallet manufacturers

Each of these is taken from the calculator as it runs today.

  • Live 3D model

    The pallet is modelled to scale as each component is selected, and the board count cannot exceed what the width allows.

  • Leader boards

    Specify wider or thicker boards on the outside edges. The gaps are recalculated and each size is costed on its own line.

  • Markup and margin

    Markup is displayed alongside the gross margin it produces, so the two are never confused.

  • Protected pricing

    Rates remain locked until you choose to edit them, which prevents accidental changes.

  • Saved presets

    Store the pallets you quote regularly and reuse them with a new quantity.

  • Quote history

    Each quote is numbered and stored with the rates it was priced on. Record it as sent, accepted or lost, or duplicate it for a repeat order.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is any installation required?

No. Pallet Quote runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and continues to work offline once loaded.

Can I use my own timber prices?

Yes. Each board and bearer size has its own price per metre, along with a price per nail. The calculator is supplied with green case pine and kiln dried sizes, and prices remain locked until you unlock them.

Does the price include labour and profit?

Yes, once they are set. Enter your labour per pallet and your markup, and each quote displays the markup alongside the gross margin it produces. Freight is not yet included.

What does it cost?

The calculator is free, with no account and no limit on the number of quotes. Pro is $29 per month in Australian dollars and adds an account, online backup and synchronisation across your devices. There is no setup fee or contract, and you can cancel at any time.

Where is my data stored?

In the browser on your device; no account is required. If you log in with your email address, your prices and quotes are also backed up online and synchronised across your devices.
